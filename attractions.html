The Core Trio

Our Three Crown Attractions

Engineered with rustic authenticity to deliver memories that last a lifetime.

Field 01
Pick-Your-Own Heritage

Heirloom Pumpkin Harvest Patch

Roam through 15 acres of vine-ripened squash. We grow over thirty specialized varieties, from Cinderella French heirlooms and ghost white Lumina pumpkins to Atlantic Giants weighing over 150 pounds.

Complimentary wheelbarrows & vine snips provided
Wash stations and pumpkin bagging assistance
Reserve Patch Time Slot
Zone 02
The Labyrinth

The 10-Acre Maize Labyrinth

Carved into 10-foot cornstalks, our 2026 puzzle features two distinct challenge circuits. The family scavenger trail takes 25 minutes, while the master labyrinth includes 12 hidden checkpoint clues and elevated observation bridges.

Interactive smartphone clue card with trivia
Twilight flashlight maze every Friday & Saturday
View Maze Challenge Info
Circuit 03
Rustic Excursion

Scenic Sunset Orchard Hayrides

Climb aboard timber wagons cushioned with fresh sweet oat straw. Pulled by restored vintage tractors, the 20-minute loop traverses apple orchards, creek crossings, and rolling hilltop vistas illuminated by sunset.

Departs every 15 minutes from the main barn
Wheelchair-accessible ADA boarding ramps
