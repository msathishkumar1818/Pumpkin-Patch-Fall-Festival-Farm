Find Your Way to Harvest Valley

Conveniently located 45 minutes outside the metro center. Follow the live interactive map below or review our step-by-step route directions.

From North & I-87

Take Exit 14 towards County Route 9 South. Continue 8.5 miles past the river crossing, turn left onto Autumn Cider Way. The farm gate is 1/2 mile on your right.

From South & Metro Area

Follow Highway 301 North for 22 miles. Merge onto Valley Road, then take the second right at the old stone barn onto Cider Way. Free parking guides will direct you.

Coach & Bus Drop-Off

Large school buses and chartered tour coaches should utilize South Farm Gate B. Dedicated paved turnaround lane and reserved staging stalls provided.
