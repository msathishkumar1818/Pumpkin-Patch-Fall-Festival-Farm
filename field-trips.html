How Your Field Trip Day Unfolds

Our streamlined itinerary ensures maximum engagement with dedicated lunch pavilions and zero wait times.

09:30 AM

Arrival & Greeter Check-In

Buses park in private bus lanes. Lead educators receive student wristbands, map packets, and assigned color group guides.

10:00 AM

Hands-On Learning Stations

Classes rotate between the bee garden, antique cider press, tractor wagon loop, and grade-appropriate corn maze challenge.

11:45 AM

Shaded Picnic Pavilion Lunch

Reserved covered pavilion tables with hand-washing stations. Students enjoy packed school lunches and fresh farm apple slices.

12:45 PM

Patch Picking & Departure

Each student hand-picks their take-home pumpkin from the field before orderly boarding back onto school buses.
